About me

Qianyu Feng is a second-year Ph.D. student at ReLER Lab, Australian Artificial Intelligence Institute (AAII), University of Technology Sydney (UTS). Her academic advisors are Prof. Yi Yang and Prof. Yunchao Wei. Her research interests include cross-domain representation learning and linguistic-vision applications. She speaks fluent in multiple languages, including Chinese, English and French. She is an advocator and member of Women in Engineering and IT. She is building AI systems aligning human-centred values and ensuring the fairness, reliability and accountability throughout their lifecycle.
